Direct backward exchanges no longer shrink the canonical under-card in place to a near-vanishing
body and then send it far out to the side. It now slides out from over the target while it recedes,
in one continuous movement, carried in the held card's shadow under an ordinary horizontal drag so
it shows no more background material than the pile does at rest. It stays a recognisable card, stands
exactly clear of the target while its depth changes, and tucks back beneath it into its exact
destination slot. Its outer reach is smaller than before and the stretch over which it is clear of
the target is wider.

Three-card Direct exchanges also keep the departing source above that under-card until the exchange
completes, so an autonomous exchange no longer briefly paints the under-card over the source at its
midpoint.
